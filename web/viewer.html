LiveKit Portal carries video and robot state in one room. This page subscribes to the camera tracks and calls the status RPC once a second. The station bridge and presentation demo are publish-only: they ignore control actions. In the presentation demo, stages, points and joint values are scripted placeholders; the video is a recording of MuJoCo simulation. A separate Director process is used for policy control.
